Exit & Billing Procedure

  1. System checks vehicle entry timestamp and calculates elapsed duration.
  2. Category hourly rate is applied (Two-Wheeler: ₹{{ "%.2f"|format(rate) }}/hr, Four-Wheeler: ₹{{ "%.2f"|format(rate) }}/hr).
  3. Any advance payment collected at entry is deducted from the total charge.
  4. Payment method and settlement are confirmed by administrator or operator.
  5. Session is marked completed, slot is instantly freed, and an official printable receipt is generated.
Security reminder: All exit transactions and receipts are signed with unique receipt numbers and stored in the SQLite database.
